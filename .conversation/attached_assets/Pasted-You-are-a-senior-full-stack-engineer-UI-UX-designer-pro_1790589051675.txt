============================================================
1. CORE PRODUCT IDEA
============================================================

Krushi Setu is a digital bridge between farmers and fair markets.

The central problem is NOT simply that farmers don't know how to grow crops.

The central problem is that farmers often lack:

- verified and understandable market prices
- access to multiple buyers
- bargaining power
- collective selling opportunities
- trustworthy buyer information
- transaction transparency
- price trends and market signals
- cost/breakeven information before selling
- accessible digital tools

Krushi Setu solves this by connecting:

FARMER
↓
MARKET INFORMATION
↓
MARKET PREPARATION
↓
VERIFIED PRICES
↓
MULTIPLE BUYERS
↓
GROUP SELLING
↓
FAIR TRANSACTION
↓
TRANSACTION PROOF


CORE PHILOSOPHY:

"Market linkage and price transparency come first."

Crop intelligence, soil intelligence, weather and disease features are supporting features that help farmers become market-ready.

Do NOT make this look like just another crop recommendation app.


============================================================
2. MAIN VALUE PROPOSITION
============================================================

The website should communicate this message clearly:

"Government market platforms already contain valuable agricultural market information. What's missing is a simple bridge that brings that information to the farmer, helps the farmer prepare economically, connects the farmer to multiple verified buyers, and records the transaction transparently."

That bridge is Krushi Setu.

Tagline:

"Sahi Fasal, Sahi Daam, Sidha Kisan Ke Naam"
